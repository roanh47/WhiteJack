# Working out rules to "Whitejack", a blackjack substitute with d12s instead of cards

Hello all!

If you're in the Burnt Owl Society, you're clearly a beautiful and well-adjusted individual. Stop reading my reddit posts.

I'm coming up with some rules to a dice game in my world called Whitejack, but I am not a smart man, so I am trying to crowd-source some intelligence. Basically it's blackjack, but played with d12s and the aim is to get to 24 or under and beat the Scar (house dealer)

What I have so far!

Round 1 - Each player places their bet. Each player and the Scar rolls 2d12 and records the result. If you roll 24, you win automatically. Well done!

Round 2 - Each player may "tick" to roll another d12 and add this to the total, or "snip" and keep their current total. Scar also rolls, unless they got 24, in which case they win and everyone elses loses.

Round 3 - More ticks or snips. If a player has rolled a total of 6d12 and still not busted, they win automatically. Scar rolls until they hit 18, 24 or busts.

Additional rules - If your first roll is a pair, you may "feint", and play each roll separately. You can place multiple bets to get access to multiple rolls, just like blackjack.

You may "Cut the fuse" after your initial two rolls. This involves doubling your bet, but you only get 1 extra "tick"

What do you think? Any and all input is appreciated.
